## Prompt lưu kèm concept

Bản mô tả dưới đây được chuẩn hóa từ yêu cầu tạo nền ở phiên trước để lưu và tái sử dụng; không phải log tool nguyên văn.

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a reusable background plate for an animated Vietnamese stick-man educational video about a steam piston and wheel, landscape 16:9, ideally 1536x864. The attached reference is STYLE AND MOOD ONLY, not an edit target: a simple hand-drawn stick figure in a richly painted warm golden environment. Generate ONLY THE EMPTY ENVIRONMENT, no characters. Scene: a cozy fictional old workshop with warm afternoon sun through a high window, painterly wood and plaster, subtly lit beams and softly blurred tools far in the background, cinematic illustrated children's animation look, textured light, depth, warm honey gold, muted teal shadows. Foreground: clear unoccupied wooden floor or low stage across the entire lower third where an animated stickman and a separate conceptual machine can stand. Central and right foreground empty and readable; avoid a prominent bench blocking feet. Corner vignette, bright natural light. No text, labels, numbers, logos, watermark, diagram, people, animals or foreground machine. Conceptual, not an accurate historical location. Render a finished background plate.

Prompt nói pít-tông/bánh xe là ý tưởng nền từ phiên trước; không cho phép thêm cơ chế đó vào bài hơi nước nếu narration không có. Nền chỉ đóng vai trò environment plate. Đặc tả hiện tại ở [STICKMAN-STORY-DIRECTION.md](../../../STICKMAN-STORY-DIRECTION.md).
