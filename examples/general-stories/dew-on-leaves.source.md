# Reference facts for dew

Water vapor in the surrounding air can condense on a surface when that surface
cools to the dew point. Nighttime cooling can therefore leave dew on surfaces
without rainfall. Dew is liquid water; frost involves ice and is a separate case.

Primary reference: [US National Weather Service: dew and frost](https://www.weather.gov/source/zhu/ZHU_Training_Page/fog_stuff/Dew_Frost/Dew_Frost.htm).
